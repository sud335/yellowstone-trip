Sat · Sept 19

Arrival in Belgrade

🌙 Late arrival🛏 Belgrade🚗 Airport transfer only
9:20 PM

Land at BZN

Collect luggage, confirm the child seat, and prioritize the rental counter if it is nearing closing time.

Safety: confirm everyone and all luggage fit before leaving the rental lot.
9:45 PM

Late dinner fallback

Copper Horse Bistro is outside security and stays open until 30 minutes after the final arrival. If everyone ate in transit, skip it and protect sleep.

10:15 PM

Drive to the Belgrade lodging

Go directly to the booked stay. Do not attempt a nighttime Yellowstone transfer.

If the flight is delayed: Pick up any available airport food, message the host, and keep the morning departure flexible.

Sun · Sept 20

Geothermal day

☀ 61° / 28°Fog early, then partly sunny🛏 West Yellowstone
7:00 AM

Leave Belgrade

Eat breakfast before departure and carry lunch, water, warm layers, and rain shells.

Road check: open the Yellowstone conditions page before leaving.
9:30 AM

Fountain Paint Pot

Start with the compact boardwalk while early fog and steam are still atmospheric rather than disruptive.

11:20 AM

Grand Prismatic Spring

By late morning, rising air temperatures should improve visibility. Stay on the boardwalk and keep the child close around thermal features.

12:50 PM

Picnic lunch

Use the packed lunch. Geyser Grill near Old Faithful is the dependable fallback.

1:40 PM

Old Faithful and Upper Geyser Basin

Check the posted prediction, see the eruption, then choose only one short boardwalk extension.

5:45 PM

Dinner in West Yellowstone

Primary: Wild West Pizzeria. Backup: The Branch.

Fog or late start: Move Grand Prismatic later. Keep Fountain Paint Pot and Old Faithful, and drop any extra basin first.

Mon · Sept 21

Wildlife + Canyon Loop

☀ 62°Near-freezing sunrise🛏 West Yellowstone
5:00 AM

Leave with breakfast packed

Fuel the night before and dress for a near-freezing start.

Wildlife: stay 100 yards from bears, wolves, and cougars, and 25 yards from bison and elk. Never stop in a travel lane.
7:45 AM

Lamar Valley

Scan the open grasslands for bison, elk, pronghorn, wolves, and bears. Look for groups with spotting scopes, then use the next legal pullout.

Allow 90 to 120 min
10:15 AM

Tower Fall or Calcite Springs

Choose one quick overlook if parking is easy. Tower Fall has a 132-foot cascade; Calcite Springs adds river and canyon views.

Allow 15 to 20 min
11:40 AM

Artist Point

Protect the classic Lower Falls view. Add Lookout Point only if the group wants a second canyon angle and parking is moving.

Allow 35 to 45 min
12:35 PM

Picnic near Canyon Village

If weather makes the picnic unpleasant, use the Canyon Lodge Eatery.

2:00 PM

Hayden Valley

Use the large pullouts for bison, elk, coyotes, and possible wolf or bear sightings. Add Mud Volcano only if energy remains.

Allow 45 to 75 min
6:30 PM

Dinner in West Yellowstone

Primary: Madison Crossing Lounge. Late backup: The Buffalo Bar.

Running late: Skip Tower Fall and Lookout Point first. If departure slips past 6:30 AM, drop Lamar and start with Artist Point, then Hayden Valley.

Tue · Sept 22

Yellowstone to Grand Teton

☀ 65° Yellowstone / 73° TetoniaMostly sunny🛏 Tetonia
6:15 AM

Check out of West Yellowstone

Carry a full picnic and start with a full fuel tank.

Road note: use Teton Park Road and US 89. The northern Moose-Wilson Road section is closed.
8:00 AM

West Thumb Geyser Basin

Look for Abyss Pool, Black Pool, Fishing Cone, and thermal features extending into Yellowstone Lake.

Allow 40 to 50 min
9:25 AM

Lewis Falls, only if parking is open

This 29-foot roadside cascade is a quick bonus, not a priority stop.

Allow 10 min
11:15 AM

Jackson Lake Dam

Walk a short section near the dam for open-water views toward Mount Moran and the northern Teton Range.

Allow 20 to 30 min
12:00 PM

Oxbow Bend + picnic

Watch for moose, otters, beavers, pelicans, ospreys, and bald eagles. Pioneer Grill is the lunch fallback.

Allow 45 to 60 min
2:00 PM

Jenny Lake Discovery Trail

The easy paved 0.35-mile trail reaches lakeshore and mountain views. Add North Jenny Lake Road only if traffic is light.

Allow 45 to 75 min
4:15 PM

Jackson Town Square

Take a short walk, then eat early at Hand Fire Pizza around 4:45 PM. Backup near the lodging: Forage Bistro.

5:45 PM

Drive to Tetonia

Cross Teton Pass before sunset and check in for the night.

Running late or low clouds: Skip Lewis Falls and North Jenny Lake Road. Pause at Jackson Lake Dam or Oxbow when the peaks appear, but protect Jenny Lake and the Tetonia arrival.

Wed · Sept 23

Mesa Falls + Yellowstone Hot Springs

🌦 63° Yellowstone / 77° PraySlight afternoon shower risk🛏 Pray
6:30 AM

Leave Tetonia

Pack swimsuits, towels, a dry bag, picnic food, rain shells, and warm layers.

Road check: confirm Yellowstone roads and the Gardner River High Bridge work zone before departure.
7:40 AM

Upper Mesa Falls

Boardwalk overlooks face the broad waterfall and volcanic cliffs along the Henrys Fork. Early arrival keeps the stop calm.

Allow 35 to 40 min
10:20 AM

Gibbon Falls

See the 84-foot waterfall from the viewing walkway only when parking is easy.

Allow 15 to 20 min
11:05 AM

Norris Porcelain Basin

Use the shorter 0.75-mile route through the park’s hottest and most dynamic thermal basin. Skip the longer Back Basin today.

Allow 45 to 55 min
1:00 PM

Mammoth Lower Terraces + lunch

Walk the lower boardwalks for changing travertine formations. Picnic to save time, or use the Mammoth Terrace Grill.

Allow 60 to 80 min
2:35 PM

Roosevelt Arch

Take the classic North Entrance photograph before continuing north.

Allow 10 to 15 min
3:00 PM

Yellowstone Hot Springs

Soak for 60 to 75 minutes in the commercial pools north of Gardiner. Regular hours are 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Tuesday through Sunday. Listed admission is $18 for ages 13+, $10 for ages 4-12, and free for ages 0-3. Towels are listed at $4.

Check current hours ↗NPS swimming guidance ↗
Use the commercial pools only. Do not enter park thermal springs. Boiling River remains unavailable and Firehole Swim Area is closed.
5:00 PM

Check in near Pray

Change and reset before dinner.

5:45 PM

Dinner in Paradise Valley

Reserve the Fireside Room at Sage Lodge. Casual backup: The Old Saloon in Emigrant.

Running late or wet: Skip Gibbon Falls first, then shorten Norris. Keep Mammoth, the North Entrance, the overnight transfer, and the soak only if roads remain safe.
