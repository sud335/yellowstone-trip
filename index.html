The full flow

Day-by-day itinerary

The pace protects the best light and views, keeps the child-friendly walks short, and leaves a comfortable flight-day margin.

19Sat

Arrival · Belgrade

Land, collect the car, and settle in

Land at BZN at 9:20 PM. Confirm the child seat and luggage fit, then drive directly to the booked Belgrade stay. Skip a nighttime Yellowstone transfer.

20Sun

Geothermal day · West Yellowstone

Fountain Paint Pot, Grand Prismatic, Old Faithful

Leave Belgrade at 7:00 AM. Put Grand Prismatic around 11:20 AM so morning fog has time to lift. Add one small basin only if everyone still has energy.

61°F · morning fog, then partly sunny

21Mon

Wildlife + canyon · West Yellowstone

Lamar Valley, Artist Point, Hayden Valley

Leave at 5:00 AM with breakfast packed. View wildlife from legal pullouts, picnic near Canyon Village, and prioritize Artist Point. If the early start is unrealistic, drop Lamar and keep Canyon plus Hayden.

62°F · mostly sunny, near-freezing start

22Tue

Yellowstone to Grand Teton · Tetonia

West Thumb, Oxbow Bend, Jenny Lake, Jackson

Check out at 6:15 AM. Protect the clearest hours for Oxbow Bend and Jenny Lake, then have an early dinner in Jackson and cross Teton Pass to Tetonia before sunset.

65°F Yellowstone · 73°F Tetonia · mostly sunny

23Wed

Scenic transfer + soak · Pray

Mesa Falls, Norris, Mammoth, Yellowstone Hot Springs

Leave Tetonia at 6:30 AM with swimsuits and towels. Visit Upper Mesa Falls, then cross Yellowstone via Norris and Mammoth. Take a Roosevelt Arch photo and soak for 60 to 75 minutes before the short drive to Pray.

63°F Yellowstone · 77°F Pray · slight PM shower risk

Running late? Skip Gibbon Falls first, then shorten Norris. Keep Mammoth, the North Entrance, and the overnight transfer.
24Thu

Flight day · BZN

Direct drive to Bozeman Yellowstone Airport

Leave Pray at 9:00 AM. Refuel near the airport, return the car around 10:20 AM, and target the terminal by 10:45 AM for the 1:35 PM flight. Do not add sightseeing.

Sunny near 79°F · south wind 8 to 13 mph

Before every departure

Road + safety checks

01

Roads

Western and southern Yellowstone routes are projected open for these dates, weather permitting. Allow construction buffers at Old Faithful, Bridge Bay, and Gardner River High Bridge.

02

Tetons

The northern Moose-Wilson Road section is closed. Use Teton Park Road and US 89. Expect a short North Park Road delay.

03

Wildlife

Stay 100 yards from bears, wolves, and cougars, and 25 yards from bison, elk, and other wildlife. Never stop in a travel lane.

04

Essentials

Keep the tank above half, carry food and water, pack warm layers and rain shells, and save all three routes offline.
